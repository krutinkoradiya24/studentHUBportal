Frequently Asked Questions

Student Hub is a centralized platform designed to provide easy access to academic information, attendance, results, and other college services.

Log in to your account, navigate to the Dashboard, and click on the 'Attendance' section in the sidebar to view your detailed subject-wise attendance.

Once results are declared, they will be uploaded to your profile. Click on 'Results' from the Quick Links or Sidebar menu to view and download them.

Go to the 'Faculty' section to see the directory of all professors and their contact details, including email and office hours.

About Student Hub

Student Hub is a centralized student portal designed to provide students with easy access to academic information and college services.

Students can manage courses, assignments, attendance, results, notices, fees, e-library and counselling from one platform.
